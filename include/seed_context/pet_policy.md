# AstroTrips Pet Travel Policy

## We love pets at AstroTrips

Pets are family, and family travels together. AstroTrips welcomes pets on
every route we fly, from a weekend Moon getaway to a six-week Titan odyssey.
Our default answer to "can my pet come?" is yes. The rest of this document
explains how we make that happen safely, what it costs, and the handful of
rules that exist purely for the wellbeing of your animal and the other
passengers on board.

If you are planning a trip and have a pet that is not covered below, talk to
us anyway. We would much rather find a way to say yes than turn you away.

## Which pets can travel

The following travel as a matter of course, no special review required:

- Cats and dogs of any breed
- Rabbits, ferrets, guinea pigs, and other small mammals
- Birds in an approved carrier
- Reptiles and amphibians in a climate-controlled enclosure
- Aquatic pets in an AstroTrips-approved sealed tank

Larger animals, working livestock, and exotic species are handled on a
per-booking basis. We have flown miniature horses, a tortoise older than the
company, and a very calm python. If you can describe how your animal eats,
sleeps, and stays comfortable, we can usually build a plan around it.

## Pet-specific rules

For some pets we have specific rules in place to ensure the safety of your pet
and our other passengers. These rules are not negotiable.

### Tribbles

- Every tribble must be examined by an AstroTrips-appointed veterinarian before
  travel. This examination is at the owner's expense and confirms, above all,
  that the tribble is not pregnant.
- A tribble must be kept in an enclosure at all times.
- A tribble may not share an enclosure with another tribble unless the
  veterinarian has confirmed that it has been neutered.

### Tardigrades

- Large tardigrades book as cargo-class with their own reserved enclosure, and
  their size counts toward vessel and shuttle payload.
- A tardigrade is one of the only animals that can survive vacuum and extreme
  radiation, but AstroTrips still requires it to travel in a life-support pod
  for insurance reasons.
- Keep your tardigrade hydrated. A dehydrated tardigrade enters a cryptobiotic
  state that looks alarmingly like death, and our crew would rather not find out
  the hard way.
- Tardigrades must remain in their enclosure and may not be connected to any
  vessel navigation system at any time.

## Pet fares

We keep pet pricing simple and friendly:

- Small pets under 10 kg travel free in the cabin with you.
- Medium pets between 10 kg and 30 kg travel for a flat modest fee per leg.
- Large and exotic animals are quoted per booking, based mainly on the space
  and life-support capacity they need.
- Service animals always travel free, on every route, with no size limit and
  priority boarding.

There is no surcharge for the number of routes or the length of the trip. A
pet fare is per booking, not per planet.

## Where pets stay

Every accommodation tier we offer is pet-friendly. You are never forced into a more expensive tier just
because you are travelling with an animal.

- Pet-friendly cabins include a secured rest area and feeding station.
- Pet suites add a dedicated play and grooming corner for travellers who want
  the extra space.
- Exercise areas are available on all vessels, with scheduled access on
  long-haul routes.

## Visiting your pet

You are welcome to visit your pet during the journey, within reason and on a
schedule that works for the crew. For most animals this means stopping by the
pet area during waking hours whenever you like.

For aquatic pets, you can visit your animal through the tank glass as often as
you wish. You cannot, however, dive into the tank. Open-tank diving is not safe
in space, and we have not yet figured out a way to make it safe. The moment we
do, you will be the first to know.

## Safety and life support

Pet safety is the one area where we are strict, because space is unforgiving.

- Every pet is fitted with a life-support-rated travel pod or harness sized to
  the animal. This is included in the pet fare.
- An onboard veterinarian is present on all long-haul routes, which today
  means Europa and Titan.
- Outer-planet trips include a short pre-flight conditioning session so your
  pet is comfortable with the pod and the change in gravity before departure.

## Documentation

We keep paperwork light and handle the hard parts for you:

- A current health certificate from your veterinarian.
- A microchip is recommended but not required.
- Interplanetary import permits are handled by AstroTrips. You do not need to
  file anything with the destination yourself.

## How many pets

You may bring up to three pets per passenger with no additional approval.
Beyond three, we just need a quick confirmation that the cabin and
life-support capacity fit, which we can usually turn around the same day.

## What changes by route and trip length

Please note: some pets, including tribbles and tardigrades, have additional
pet-specific rules of their own, on top of anything below. See the "Pet-specific
rules" section before booking.

The shorter and closer the trip, the simpler the requirements:

- Moon and Venus getaways of three to five days have minimal requirements
  beyond the health certificate.
- Mars expeditions of one to three weeks add a food and medication
  provisioning plan so we carry enough for the whole trip.
- Europa and Titan long-haul voyages of fourteen days or more add a
  veterinary check, an exercise schedule, extra provisioning, and the
  pre-flight conditioning session noted above.

## Shuttle legs and weight limits

If your itinerary includes a shuttle leg, for example a layover on a
multi-leg trip, your pet's weight counts toward the shuttle payload. Large
animals may need their own reserved space on the shuttle, which can affect the
shuttle cost. See the multi-leg trips policy for how shuttle pricing works.

## The few rules

These exist for safety and for the comfort of other travellers:

- A pet must be one we can safely life-support for the full journey. If we
  cannot guarantee that, we will tell you honestly rather than risk the animal.
- Some destinations require a short quarantine on arrival or on return. We will
  flag this before you book.
- Animals with a history of aggression need a brief behaviour review so we can
  plan cabin placement.

## Changes and cancellations

- Adding a pet to an existing booking is easy and can be done up to departure,
  subject to capacity.
- If you need to cancel because of a pet health issue, our standard
  cancellation policy applies, and our team will help you rebook.

## Questions

Reach the AstroTrips pet travel desk and we will help you plan a trip your
whole family, including the four-legged and the scaled, will enjoy.
